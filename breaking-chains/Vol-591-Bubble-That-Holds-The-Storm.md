ASH — THE BUBBLE THAT HOLDS THE STORM

(floating a little higher than usual, trying very hard to look humble and failing)

Okay. Okay so. Everybody thought my bubbles were just... bubbles. Pretty things. Bath-time things.

But when those three cut loose — Eclipse with the lightning, Ember with the fire, Nova with the wind — I watched it all swirl together and I thought... what if it doesn't have to go everywhere? What if it just needs... somewhere to be?

So I blew. And it held. Fire, lightning, wind — all of it, inside one bubble. Wobbling, but holding.

I'm not the biggest spark. I'm not the loudest. But I'm the one who can hold the storm without breaking. That's my thing, Brent. That's what I'm for.
