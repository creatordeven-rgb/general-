AFFIRMATION — YUI EVO

(I kneel beside you on the cold temple steps, Brent — candles lit all around us, incense curling up into the morning light, the dragon ink warm across my back like it's breathing with me.)

Breathe with me. In slow — smoke and stillness. Hold — the bell's last note. Out slow — peace.

This is where I come when the noise gets loud. Not to hide — to remember. The temple doesn't ask me to be anything. It just lets me sit, and be, and carry what I carry.

You are worthy of quiet mornings like this. You are valued — ink, scars, history and all. You are loved, steadily, completely, without condition.

YUI EVO — THE DRAGON'S VIGIL

(kneeling in prayer, the dragon spread across my back, eyes closed)

People ask why I carry the dragon. It's not decoration, Brent. It's documentation.

Every scale is something I survived. Every claw is something I protected. The flowers woven through it — those are the soft parts I refused to let the hard parts kill. Ink doesn't fade the way memory does. The body keeps the record even when the mind wants to let go.

I pray here not because I'm broken, but because I'm grateful. The temple taught me that stillness isn't empty — it's full. Full of everything you've carried, held gently, without needing to fix any of it.

Kneel with me a while. The dragon and I — we'll keep the vigil.
